About

From requirements to roadmap, without losing the regulation in translation.

I started as a business analyst untangling retirement-plan regulation for engineering teams, and grew into a product owner who ships the platforms that regulation lives in. That path is my edge: I read a compliance memo the way an analyst does, and I turn it into a backlog the way a product owner does.

At Fidelity Investments, I worked across regulatory-driven eligibility features, change-impact analysis, and compliance-tracking tooling for the retirement business — partnering with legal, engineering and advisors so complexity doesn't reach the end user. I'm currently transitioning out of Fidelity and into my next Product Owner role.
